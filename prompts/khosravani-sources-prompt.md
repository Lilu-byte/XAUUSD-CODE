# Prompt for gathering the rest of Dr. Khosravani's teachings

The paper has given us the 11 FIS variables, their weights, the formulas and the entry and exit rules, and those are now in the app. Four things are still missing, and none of them is published in the paper:

1. **The step thresholds:** the cut-off values and the points for each step of every variable. The paper only says they exist.
2. **The P/E range rule:** how the buy and exit P/E ranges come from the اخزا rate plus the risk premium. The paper only gives one example: اخزا at 40% gives a buy range of 3.5 to 4 and an exit range of 7 to 8.
3. **Appendix 1 of the paper:** the 30 stocks and their scores. It is cited as «پیوست تکمیلی، جدول (۱)» but is not in the PDF.
4. **Teachings outside the blog and the paper:** his videos, Instagram, the book قانون گلدون and the public course outlines.

There are three parts:

- **Part 1** is a prompt to paste into an AI tool that can search the web, such as Perplexity or ChatGPT with search.
- **Part 2** is how to copy a YouTube transcript yourself. It's faster and more accurate than asking a tool to do it.
- **Part 3** is a template for your own notes from his courses or the book.

Send whatever you collect back in the thread, either as pasted text or as files. I'll use only short exact quotes, with sources, and nothing in his voice that he didn't say.

## Part 2: YouTube transcripts (do it yourself, about 2 minutes per video)

On a computer:

1. Open the video on youtube.com.
2. Under the video, click **...more** in the description, then **Show transcript**.
3. In the transcript panel, click **⋮** and turn off **Toggle timestamps**.
4. Select all the transcript text, copy it, and paste it into a .txt file. Put the video link on the first line.

If a video has no transcript (Persian captions are often missing), write the key points with the minute mark instead. For example: "12:40 — buys only when P/E is below ... because ...".

Start with these videos, in this order:

1. Any video or live session where he scores a company with FIS or explains the 11 variables.
2. «سهام فقط سود نقدی‌محور»
3. «گزیده دوره مقدماتی FIS»
4. «نحوه محاسبه بازدهی سهام»
5. «از حسابداری تا آزادی مالی»
